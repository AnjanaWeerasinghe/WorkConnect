Problem Brief

The Problem We're Solving

Finding and hiring reliable home service workers is a broken experience for both sides

🔍
No Centralised Discovery
Customers rely on word-of-mouth or unverified classifieds to find plumbers, electricians, and tradespeople — with no quality guarantee.
💸
Opaque Pricing & No Digital Payments
No standard way to agree on a price upfront and no secure digital payment method — leading to disputes and cash-only complications.
📍
Zero Real-time Visibility
Once a worker is booked, customers have no way to track where they are or when they will arrive, causing frustration and missed appointments.
🛡️
No Verification or Trust System
Anyone can claim to be skilled. There is no vetting process, no reviews, and no accountability — making hiring risky for customers.

Aim & Objectives

What We Set Out to Build

A clear mission backed by six measurable development objectives

🎯 Project Aim

To design and develop a cross-platform mobile & web application that digitises the home service industry by connecting customers with verified workers — enabling real-time tracking, transparent pricing, and secure payment processing.

Inspired by: The Uber model applied to home services — on-demand, location-aware, and fully digital.
O1
Build a job request system where customers post service needs with location, description & budget
O2
Implement real-time GPS tracking so customers can follow a worker's live location on a map
O3
Integrate Stripe payment processing supporting both card (online) and cash payment flows
O4
Develop a worker dashboard to manage incoming jobs, navigate to customers & track earnings
O5
Create a review and star-rating system with automatic average updates via Cloud Functions
O6
Deliver an admin panel for worker registration vetting — approve or reject with written reason

System Flow

How It Works

A seamless two-sided marketplace — from job request to payment

🧑‍💼 Customer Flow
1
Browse services & submit a job request with location & budget
2
Track the assigned worker in real-time on a live map
3
Worker completes the job & marks it done in the app
4
Customer selects Cash or Card to pay, then leaves a review
⇄
🔧 Worker Flow
1
Receive a job request notification & accept the job
2
Navigate to the customer's location via Google Maps
3
Complete the work, then tap Complete Job in the app
4
Earnings & star ratings update automatically on the dashboard
